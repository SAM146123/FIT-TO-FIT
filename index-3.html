Everything for a fitter day

One app instead of five. It works with your phone's own step counter and Health Connect.

Steps, distance and calories

Your daily steps are read from Health Connect and shown with a goal ring and weekly bars.

GPS walk, run, cycle, hike

Track a route with time, pace and speed. Pause and resume any time.

Video coach workouts

Follow guided exercises with a timer and see what each workout did for your body.

Diet plans for health conditions

Diabetes, BP, cholesterol, thyroid, TB, joint pain, hair, skin and eye care.

First aid and home tips

Simple home remedies and first aid steps, in English and Tamil.

Smart reminders

Water, medicine and hourly step-goal nudges until you reach your target.

Weight, sugar and BP records

Log readings, see trends on a chart and share them as text.

Friends challenge

Add friends with a code and compete on a daily and weekly leaderboard.

Badges and levels

Earn badges for streaks, 10K days, workouts and hydration.

AI assistant and food scan

Use your own free Gemini key to scan a meal photo or ask health questions.

Install in 3 steps

Takes about a minute.

  1. Download the APKTap the red Download button above. The file is called FitToFit.apk.
  2. Allow installing from this sourceAndroid will ask once. Tap Settings, then switch on Allow from this source. The app is not on the Play Store yet, so Android shows this for every app installed from a file.
  3. Install and openIf Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details, then Install anyway. Open FIT TO FIT, create your profile and allow Health Connect.

Why the warning? Play Protect warns about apps that are not from the Play Store. FIT TO FIT is open on GitHub and updates install over the old version without losing your data.

Privacy

Plain answers about your data.

What the app stores on your phone

Your profile, workout and food logs, weight, sugar and BP records, reminders and settings stay on your phone.

What goes online

Only if you sign in: your name, photo, steps and level are stored in Firebase so friends can see the leaderboard. Cloud backup is optional and private to your account.

Permissions

Location for GPS tracking, activity and Health Connect for steps, notifications for reminders. You can turn each off in Android settings.

AI features

The AI assistant uses your own Gemini key. Your questions go to Google Gemini only when you ask. Nothing is sold or used for ads.

Health information

Tips and diet advice are general guidance, not medical advice. See a doctor for any health problem. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
